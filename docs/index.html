Notable hiring signals

Evidence-cited findings from this snapshot. Job postings are hiring signals — they suggest priorities, they don't prove internal strategy.

  1. Infrastructure skills lead the industry.

    Observability (28.5%), Kubernetes (25.6%), Terraform (15.7%) and CI/CD (13.5%) all rank in the top ten — managed WordPress hosting recruits like a cloud-infrastructure industry that happens to serve WordPress.

  2. WordPress itself appears in 28.1% of vacancies.

    Concentrated in the pure-play hosts: 29 of Pantheon's 30 jobs, all 19 at Automattic, all 11 at WP Engine. The diversified companies mention it far less.

  3. AI hiring is real but concentrated.

    LLMs appear in 46 jobs (16.4%) — 42 of them at DigitalOcean and GoDaddy. The pure-play WordPress hosts show almost no LLM-related hiring yet.

  4. One company is half the dataset.

    DigitalOcean accounts for 143 of 281 analysed jobs (51%), so sector-level rates lean toward its profile; company-level tables give the cleaner pure-play picture.
